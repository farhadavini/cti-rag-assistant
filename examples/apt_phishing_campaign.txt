Threat Intelligence Report - Spear-Phishing Campaign "QuietHarbor"
Classification: TLP:CLEAR (example / training data)
Date: 2026-01-14

Summary
-------
A spear-phishing campaign tracked as QuietHarbor targeted finance departments of
mid-sized European manufacturers between November 2025 and January 2026. The
actor used invoice-themed lures to deliver a lightweight PowerShell downloader.

Initial Access
--------------
Victims received emails with a password-protected ZIP attachment. The archive
contained a shortcut (.lnk) that invoked powershell.exe with an encoded command
(-EncodedCommand). The downloader retrieved a second stage from a compromised
WordPress site over HTTPS.

Execution & Defense Evasion
---------------------------
- T1059.001 PowerShell: base64-encoded one-liner, AMSI bypass attempt via
  reflection on System.Management.Automation.AmsiUtils.
- T1027 Obfuscation: string concatenation and format-operator tricks.
- T1140 Deobfuscation: gzip-compressed payload decoded in memory.

Command & Control
-----------------
The second stage beaconed to a single IP over port 443 at regular 60-second
intervals (low jitter), a strong beaconing indicator. No domain was used; the
raw IP suggests throwaway infrastructure.

Recommendations
---------------
- Block execution of .lnk files from archive attachments.
- Enable PowerShell ScriptBlock logging and alert on -EncodedCommand.
- Monitor for regular-interval outbound connections to raw IPs on 443.
